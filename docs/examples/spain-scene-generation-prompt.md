## Rules for every room pair

1. Generate one complete environment per image. No collages, contact sheets, split views, before/after panels, or collections of miniatures
2. Generate a wide composition, ideally 16:9, and an independently composed portrait, ideally 9:16, at the largest native size available. If the tool supports different native ratios, use the closest appropriate wide and tall canvases and report the dimensions. Do not crop, stretch, or upscale one to make the other
3. Keep the same place, food identity, palette, and main characters across the pair. Rearrange the composition for portrait rather than squeezing the wide layout into it
4. Use a human-height or slightly elevated three-quarter view that reveals the food surface and the surrounding place. The main food should occupy a substantial, readable part of the foreground; avoid tiny food lost in scenery
5. Keep the three listed discovery subjects visible, distinct, and reachable in both orientations. Leave breathing room around them; do not hide them behind hands, table edges, steam, or decorations
6. Build foreground food, middle-ground activity, and background place into one coherent scene. Keep roughly four to eight clearly readable people, with smaller background figures only where useful
7. Vary ages, bodies, faces, clothing, occupations, and gestures. Show locals cooking, choosing, carrying, serving, and sharing. Use restrained historic or traditional everyday workwear suited to the setting, with varied aprons, shirts, skirts, waistcoats, and headwear where appropriate. Do not turn everyone into the same festival performer or imply that all these foods belong to one ancient era
8. Make the painting complete without extra sprites: Include its static furniture, utensils, food, people, buildings, and plants. Every seat and work surface must support what rests on it
9. Give each room a clear food-preparation area that Codex can animate locally. Keep its edges crisp and its activity small enough to fit the picture
10. Do not paint interface elements into the art: No diamond markers, arrows, hotspot rings, text panels, captions, filenames, borders, watermarks, or readable invented menus. Codex adds the small diamond markers over the actual subjects later

Use white stucco, terracotta, warm stone, wrought iron, ceramic tile, orange and olive trees, restrained mosaics, and outdoor cafés where they fit. Northern rooms should have their own stone, timber, greenery, and cooler coastal light. Gaudí-inspired forms belong to ES09's surroundings; white courtyards to ES07; dry fields and windmills to ES10.

## The ten room briefs

### ES01 — Paella courtyard

**Files:** `es01_paella_wide.png`, `es01_paella_portrait.png`

- **Place and composition:** A Valencia-inspired outdoor rice kitchen under a reed shade, with warm paving, an orange tree, shared lunch tables, and a modest opening towards coastal rice-growing country. The paella pan is the largest food object in the entire set, at a believable cooking scale
- **Food:** Put a broad, shallow Valencian-style pan in the foreground with chicken, rabbit, flat green beans, and large white garrofó beans. A smaller, separate seafood paella pan can sit at the neighbouring station with mussels and prawns. Keep the two preparations visually distinct. Do not fill the Valencian pan with a mixture of seafood and every Spanish ingredient
- **Wide / portrait:** Wide shows the two cooking stations and communal tables. Portrait stacks the hero pan, cook, shaded diners, and landscape vertically; keep the main pan's handles and food surface visible
- **Three discovery subjects:** Rice grains in a small scoop; the cooked edge of the hero pan; a clearly visible green-bean-and-garrofó ingredient bowl
- **Motion plan:** Rice gently simmers in the cooking pan; a small amount of steam rises; one leaf or distant coastal cue can move. Do not show a cook constantly stirring finished paella like risotto
- **Story research:** Rice-growing landscapes, the pan and cooking method, and the distinction between Valencian and seafood versions. Use the regional recipe as a visual ingredient check: [Comunitat Valenciana](https://www.comunitatvalenciana.com/en/gastronomic-tourism/recipes/paella-valenciana)

### ES02 — Plaza tapas bar

**Files:** `es02_tapas_wide.png`, `es02_tapas_portrait.png`
