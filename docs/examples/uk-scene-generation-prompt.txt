We are creating production-quality illustrated scene assets for a project called Food World.

This world represents **UK food culture**, with a strong focus on everyday British food, regional traditions, pubs, bakeries, tea, roasts, pies, fish and chips, markets, coastal food, breakfasts, Indian-British influence, seasonal produce and family meals.

The world should feel culturally broad enough to reflect the UK significantly, while avoiding the mistake of reducing British food to only fish and chips, tea and roast dinner.

The visual world should communicate:

hearth  
rain  
warm interiors  
pub culture  
baking  
tea  
roasts  
pies  
coastal food  
markets  
seasonality  
comfort food  
multicultural cities  
family meals

CORE VISUAL LANGUAGE

Use:

- warm brick
- dark timber
- cream plaster
- weathered stone
- slate grey
- deep green
- burgundy
- mustard
- navy
- copper and brass accents
- cozy window light
- rain outside warm interiors
- pub woodwork
- market stalls
- bakery counters
- tiled cafés
- village streets
- coastal harbours
- allotments
- rolling green countryside
- hedgerows
- old stone walls
- fireplaces
- enamelware
- ceramic mugs
- cast iron
- wooden tables
- chalkboards used sparingly
- patterned pub carpets only if subtle

Avoid:

- making every scene look Victorian
- excessive Union Jack decoration
- royal / palace imagery
- generic Harry Potter fantasy styling
- stereotypes like red phone boxes everywhere
- turning every room into a pub
- overly twee cottage aesthetics
- making all UK food brown and heavy

The world should feel lived-in, contemporary enough to be recognizable, but rooted in long food traditions.

IMPORTANT PRODUCTION RULES

1. Generate every full room or environment as its own image.
2. Never combine multiple rooms into a contact sheet or collage.
3. Use the largest native output size available.
4. Generate portrait and wide versions independently.
5. Do not crop or upscale one image to create another.
6. No captions, filenames, borders or checkerboards inside images.
7. For props, only generate items that are useful for animation, interaction, or scene layering.
8. Prop background must be pure plain white.
9. One object or one tightly related small group per image.
10. Make each prop large enough that its short side is at least 400 px when extracted.
11. If an asset is too small, regenerate it rather than resizing afterwards.

ANIMATION PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------

The full room paintings must already feel complete.

Separate assets exist only where motion adds value.

Prioritize:

steam  
fire  
pouring  
rain  
wind  
boat movement  
food preparation  
oven glow  
small character actions

Do not generate static bowls, chairs, plates or decorations separately unless they need to move or be clickable.

Generate one full room at a time.

Then generate only the reduced animation asset set.

Never combine multiple requested outputs into one asset sheet.
