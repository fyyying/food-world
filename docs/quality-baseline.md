## What the harnesses enforce

These checks were written after a defect reached the owner. They live in `scripts/tests/spain-world.mjs` and `scripts/tests/room-loops.mjs` and run in `npm test`. **Every new area's `<id>-world.mjs` carries the world checks**; copy them and change the ids. A check that a new area cannot satisfy is a design decision the lead records here, not a check to drop.

| Enforced | Where | Added because |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nothing decorative stands between a stand and the camera.** For every clickable object, a 100-degree wedge centred on the direction from the stand to the default camera stays clear for 9 units of anything with a body — a house, the Basque farmhouse, an arcade, a walled pen, the statue, or any decor over 2 units tall — and clear for 5 units of anything over 1.2 units tall. A slender post under .6 of a unit across either way is exempt, because it hides nothing at this camera pitch | `spain-world.mjs` | Five stands were completely behind houses after the line check below had passed. A ray is not a rule: the camera can be swung 43 degrees either way, so the whole fan has to be clear |
| **No stand stands in front of another stand.** Ten rays per clickable object along the arrival direction — nine at its camera-facing front face at three heights, one at the diamond cue over its anchor — and the first stand each ray meets must be that stand. A wedge cannot express this one: the stands are 4 x 4 to 12 x 14 and neighbours inside a cluster are five to eight units apart, so every pair would sit inside every other pair's wedge. What the visitor sees is settled by occlusion, so occlusion is what is measured | `spain-world.mjs` | The owner's rule is that every clickable object is visible from the camera. Ten of Spain's 26 were behind a neighbouring stand once the houses and trees were cleared |
| **The overview is readable at the world's own zoom-out limit.** The fog's far plane is computed from the limit plus half the table's diagonal, so the table centre is under 10 percent hazed at the limit and the far corner under 40. A world whose limit is inside the old 90/200 pair keeps 90 and 200 exactly | `spain-world.mjs`, `world-camera.ts` | The Mediterranean used 90/200 with a 215 limit, so at maximum zoom-out the whole table was past the far plane and rendered flat in the paper colour, with only the sea visible |
| **No two stand footprints share ground.** A footprint is the ground-plane box of everything a stand is made of that stands on the ground; a mesh starting above 2.5 units is left out. Two stands keep a unit of clear ground. Eighteen pairs cannot and are listed in the harness with the overlap measured on 2026-09-17: the list is a ceiling, so a listed pair may not get worse by more than .05 and an unlisted pair may not appear | `spain-world.mjs` | The owner saw the paddies and the huerta beds "overlapped a bit and show both in a section". Twenty-six pairs were under a unit, twenty-three of them overlapping, the worst by 5.8 |
| A stand's footprint box stays off the water (the moored port and one bank overhang held at a ceiling), and every stand has a road centreline under or beside its footprint. A blanket "no footprint crosses a road" cannot hold: every door is within 2.6 of a road and the stands are 5 to 12 units across, so 25 of 26 have their own road under them by construction | `spain-world.mjs` | Measured on 2026-09-17 while adding the footprint rule |
| Decorative houses stay between 4 and 8 for the area, one or two per style, bounded at both ends, and the styles that must still have a house are named. Castile and Valencia must have none | `spain-world.mjs` | Twenty-four houses hemmed the stands in and closed three lanes; fourteen still stood in front of twelve stands. The wedge rule above is what decides where a house may stand, and on this table it leaves room for five |
| Nothing solid stands on a road centreline; every centreline is sampled every 0.25 units against every solid decor footprint. Only a bridge deck is walked over | `spain-world.mjs` | Six houses, two sheaves, a rock, an outcrop and an apple tree stood on lanes |
| Every stand keeps an open corridor from the nearest point of the road network, with 2.5 units clear in front of the stand | `spain-world.mjs` | Stands could not be walked up to |
| No house or decor stands on the arrival camera's line to a stand, from any azimuth the stand can be approached from. Kept as well as the wedge above: this one casts the real approach ray at the reacting subject inside a stand, the wedge keeps the ground in front of the whole stand clear | `spain-world.mjs`, `spain-reactions.mjs` | A three-storey house stood on the line to two stands |
| Gait: any figure whose world position travels must swing its thighs, and a figure that should stand still must not travel. A figure carried by a vehicle must be seated | `spain-world.mjs` | Ten paced walkers slid with locked legs; three sailors stood upright on sailing boats |
| Gait direction: an animal's head axis leads its travel, and its hooves spend clearly more of the stride going backward under the body than forward | `spain-world.mjs` | The mill mule circled tail-first with pendulum legs |
| Every hung lamp reaches the ground and carries its lantern above head height | `spain-world.mjs` | Six square lamps hung in the air after the arcades were removed |
| Removed decor stays removed: the arcade, the barraca, the hórreo and the Basque farmhouse must be absent | `spain-world.mjs` | Each read from the overview as something it is not, or stood in front of clickable objects |
| Chimney smoke anchors sit at the chimney top, computed from each style's own ridge height plus the cap; a smoking house produces at least four puff sprites and uses the tinted variant | `spain-world.mjs` | Smoke was born inside the roof geometry and was invisible at overview zoom |
| A swaying hung sprite counts as a loop, so a room that replaces a keyed crop with a sprite keeps three or four loops in both orientations | `room-loops.mjs` | The eleven Spain breeze crops were removed |

Conventions these rest on:

- **A room picture may be a derived file.** A painted object that a sprite will replace is painted out offline by `scripts/scenes/paint-out-strings.py`, which writes the original and a SHA-256 sidecar to `.data/originals/<room>/<orientation>.jpg` and refuses to run twice over the same file. `--restore` undoes it. `.data` is outside git, so the reproducible chain is the delivered PNGs (hashes in `public/scenes/<area>-assets.json`) → `import-<id>.py` → `paint-out-strings.py`. Four Spain JPGs are derived today
- **Hanging motion is a sprite over a clean painting**, the way the China hotpot room hangs its lanterns. New areas do not colour-key a crop out of a finished painting; the thirty existing crops in China, Xinjiang and Turkey stay. A breeze-mask coverage above about 0.7 means the key is taking the wall
- **Every delivered motion sprite is used, or its non-use is explained in the area's room doc**

Known gaps, accepted for now:

- Levant, Arabia, Persia, Greece, Morocco and Dalmatia have card-only objects and no rooms. They predate the China standard; the Mediterranean audit began on 2026-09-16 with Spain
- The Everyday area of China has one room and one card-only object
- Hotpot's people wear present-day clothes; the art direction exempts it as the light and density reference
- Portrait composites from `__fw.sceneShot` squash the frame in a hidden pane; judge portrait rooms in the live pane at 390 x 844

How to update: change a value, add one line under a dated heading below that says what changed and why, and commit the file with the change that caused it.
