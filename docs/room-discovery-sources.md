# Painted-room discoveries

The room touch points in `src/fw/scene-props.ts` use short, original explanations of ingredients, cooking techniques and the surrounding landscape. A fact describes a practice or mechanism; it does not claim that every pictured dish uses that method. Animal responses remain simple acknowledgements.

Sources checked for specialist claims:

- Chilli heat is produced in the seed-bearing tissue, not the seeds: [New Mexico State University, The Wonders of New Mexico Chile](https://irrigationmanagement.nmsu.edu/documents/crop-description.pdf).
- Gluten development, yeast and rising dough: [Exploratorium, Bread Science 101](https://annex.exploratorium.edu/cooking/bread/bread_science.html).
- Green and black tea share a plant species; processing changes the tea: [UK Tea & Infusions Association, Tea FAQs](https://www.tea.co.uk/tea-faqs).
- Rice-wine starters break down starch for fermentation: [FAO, Fermented cereals, chapter 3](https://www.fao.org/4/x2184e/x2184e09.htm).
- Alcoholic and acetic fermentation are separate stages of vinegar production: [FAO, Fermented fruits and vegetables, chapter 5](https://www.fao.org/4/x0560e/x0560e10.htm).
- Cumin “seeds” are fruits: [Kew, Cuminum cyminum](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A840882-1/general-information).
- Mitten crabs have hairy claws: [Natural History Museum, Mitten Crab Watch](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/take-part/monitor-and-encourage-nature/mitten-crab-watch.html).
- Lotus rhizomes and water-repellent leaves: [Kew, Sacred lotus](https://www.kew.org/plants/sacred-lotus).
- Autumn pigments and nutrient recovery: [National Park Service, Why leaves change colour](https://home.nps.gov/articles/why-do-leaves-change-in-the-fall.htm).
- Underground irrigation in Turpan: [UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Karez Wells](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5347/).
- The mountain and oasis landscape: [UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Xinjiang Tianshan](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1414).

The water and leaf effects are decorative responses anchored to the existing painting, not simulations. They should finish and clean up, and must never move a whole painted object or obscure a food detail.
