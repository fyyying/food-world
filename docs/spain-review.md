# Spain review: Stage D walk and Stage E against the definition of done

Independent review of the Spain area of the Mediterranean world, 2026-09-16. The reviewer wrote none of the files
under review. This document records; it changes nothing. One table per section of the definition of done in
[the team playbook](agent-team-playbook.md) section 5, one row per line, with the evidence and, for each failure,
the owning role and the exact fix.

How the evidence was gathered. Mechanical: `npm run typecheck`, `npm test`, `npm run build:pages`,
`node scripts/audit/objects.mjs`, `node scripts/audit/flicker.mjs` at each of the six cluster centres with radius 12,
`uv run --with pillow --with numpy --with scipy scripts/audit/breeze-masks.py`, and
`uv run --with pillow --with numpy scripts/audit/room-motion.py`. Browser: the dev server on port 5180 at
1280 x 720 and 390 x 844, entering through `__fw.enter('mediterranean')` with `window.__fwInstant = true`, visiting
every cluster centre at distance 36 and 16 from both ends of the camera's azimuth range, clicking all twenty-seven
objects, and opening all twelve rooms in both orientations. Screenshot names below are files in `.data/shots`.

## Summary

Spain is close to the China standard and the parts that are hardest to get right are right: six dense clusters,
eleven continuous roads that all connect, one unbroken water surface with square caps, twenty walkers who never
cross a wall or wade, twenty-seven stands whose click chain is proved in order and returns to rest, twelve rooms
with measured coordinates in both orientations, and cards with dated records, labelled legends and real sources.

One stale line in a test file hides most of that work: `spain-world.mjs` fails on its second assertion because the
desktop zoom limit changed from 90 to 215 for the grown table, so the harness that guards the roads, the water,
the buildings and the walkers never runs. Correcting that one expectation makes the whole file pass.

The defect a visitor would actually see is the approach camera. The code that lowers the pitch so roofs cannot hide
the food is overridden by the OrbitControls polar clamp, so every approach arrives at 26 degrees of elevation, and
at that angle the churrería and the courtyard kitchen are invisible from both ends of the camera's 86 degree
azimuth range, and six more stands are blocked from one end.

Two smaller things break rules outright: the trencadís terrace and bench hang over the strait with nothing under
them, and three wide or portrait rooms fall below the visible-motion floor when measured through the world route
for the first time.

Nothing in the area is dishonest, unsourced or invented, no console error appeared in the whole walk, and the
Mediterranean is correctly still asleep on the public page while the owner decides about Greece, Morocco and
Dalmatia.

## Overall verdict

FAIL at Stage E, with ten of the twenty-five lines of the definition of done not true: water and floating objects
in the World section, reaction readability in the Stands section, visible motion, the manchego portrait breeze mask
and unverified reduced motion in the Rooms section, and all four lines of the Verification record. The Cards and
stories section passes in full. None of the failures needs new pictures or new research; the largest is a one-line
test correction, the next is a camera change in `main.ts`, and the rest are position and tuning work inside files
the existing owners already hold. The area should return to the Builder, the Stand maker, the Room maker and the
Lead, and Stage E should repeat before the owner is asked about publishing.
