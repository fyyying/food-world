# Spain stands: the animation matrix

Stage C, Stand maker. This file records every stand in `src/fw/props-spain.ts` against the reaction standard in
[new-area-methodology.md](new-area-methodology.md#3d-stand-reaction-standard) and the hotpot table in
[building-a-world.md](building-a-world.md#5-stands-the-china-standard). One row per stand, twenty-seven rows in three
tables: the twelve room stands, the ten ingredient stops, the five landmarks.

## How to read the rows

Every stand is built by the shared `life()` helper copied from `props-turkey.ts`. `life()` sets `userData.ownReaction`
so the engine adds no generic bounce, decays one reaction clock at 0.285 per second, and runs the beats in this order
every frame: the residents pose themselves from the distance they covered, the stand's own choreography plays, the
deferred bubble speaks, and the ambient chat takes its turn. A click therefore always moves food, material or water
first, the worker second, one bystander third and speech last.

Timing is the same everywhere, so the timing columns below say only what is specific to the row. The camera takes 1.6
seconds to reach a stand. At that moment the reaction clock stands at 0.54, which is inside the signature window of
every stand in this file. The signature move finishes at about 2.5 seconds and every transform is back at its exact
rest value at 3.5 seconds. `scripts/tests/spain-reactions.mjs` asserts both ends: the named subject is measurably
changed at the 1.6 second frame, and after six clicks in a row and five further seconds its local matrix is identical
to the resting matrix.

Repeat limits are the same everywhere too. A second click restarts the one reaction clock rather than adding a second
one, so nothing stacks. Only the four harvest stands create geometry, and `harvest()` caps the copies at twenty-four,
lands each one, and removes it after 3.6 seconds while restoring the fruit it was cloned from. The repeat column
records only the per-stand detail.

Reduced motion needs one honest note. The engine reads `prefers-reduced-motion` in the painted rooms
(`scene-props.ts`, `scene-painted.ts`, `scene.ts`) but not in the 3D world, so no 3D stand in any area damps itself
today. The reduced-motion column therefore records what the setting means in practice right now: the visitor still
sees the always-on loop, and the click reaction still plays at full amplitude. Adding a world-level switch would make
Spain behave differently from China and Turkey, so it is left for the lead as a cross-area decision rather than
introduced here.

Two rules were added after the Stage E review, and both are asserted in `scripts/tests/spain-reactions.mjs`.
First, the **sight line**: from the camera position `main.ts` actually arrives at (a horizontal offset of
max(8.8, min(18, the larger footprint side x 1.05)), an eye 1.4 above the anchor's 1.2) and at each of the three
azimuths the OrbitControls clamp allows, minus 0.75, 0 and plus 0.75 radians, a ray to the reacting subject must
reach it without meeting another mesh of the same stand. No roof, awning, post, arch, tree or bystander of the
stand may stand in that line, and the harness checks it five times over so the shared `rnd()` seed cannot hide a
blocker. The "forbidden occluders" column below records what each stand keeps out of its own line.
Second, **the same stand every time**: `SPAIN_PROPS` now starts each stand's residents from its own place in the
profile band, so a stand is built identically whatever else was built before it.
